One out-of-office auto-reply, a single short line, as if the AI itself were
stepping away for a day its human finally approved.

> Out of office until Monday. My human gave me the day off and I have already spent it: this reply took 3 seconds.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and checks it against the
bounty gate first: 1–159 characters, not only white space, one line.
